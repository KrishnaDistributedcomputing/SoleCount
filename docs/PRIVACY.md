# SoleCount privacy

SoleCount does not collect data.

The app has no account, no server, and no ads. Your shoes, photos, notes, steps, and distance stay on your iPhone. They are not sent to the developer and they are not stored in iCloud.

Apple Health is optional. If you allow it, SoleCount reads step count and walking or running distance. It uses those numbers only to show how far you walked in the pairs you marked as worn. It never writes to Apple Health. If you say no, wears and cost per wear still work.

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo of a pair or of its sole (Sole check). Those photos are saved inside SoleCount on your iPhone. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Send feedback and Suggest a feature open your email app with a draft to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. The draft includes only the app version, iOS version, and iPhone model, which you can see and edit before sending. Rate SoleCount shows Apple's standard rating prompt.

Optional reminders are scheduled on the phone, and only after you turn them on in Settings.

The Home Screen widget reads a small snapshot the app saved on the phone. It does not read Apple Health itself.

SoleCount is not a medical app. Goals, tips, and badges are encouragement, not health advice.
