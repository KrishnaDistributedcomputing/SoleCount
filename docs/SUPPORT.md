# SoleCount support

SoleCount is a free iPhone app. There is no account and no purchase.

Your pairs, photos, and Apple Health readings stay on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded. Health is optional and read-only. The app still works if you do not allow Health.

SoleCount is not a medical app.

If something looks wrong, email krishnaazure1975@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and the iOS version if you can.
