Walk more, track every km your sneakers take you. SoleCount turns your collection into simple numbers: how often you wear each pair, what every wear costs, and how far you walked in them.

TAP ONCE, SEE THE VALUE
• Add a pair with photos, brand, type, size, colorway, price, and purchase date
• Pick from a built-in list of popular brands and models
• Tap "Wore today" whenever you wear them
• Cost per wear updates instantly (price ÷ wears)

APPLE HEALTH
• Read steps and walking distance for the days you wore each pair
• See kilometres (or miles) per shoe, a daily step ring, and a weekly goal
• Set a replacement distance and get a gentle nudge when a pair has done its job
• Nothing is sent off your iPhone, and nothing is written to Apple Health

KEEP MOVING
• A Today page with a gentle streak, rest days, badges, and a short original tip
• A green, yellow, or red bar shows how close a pair is to its distance target
• Retire a pair without deleting it. Stats stay in the Hall of Fame
• Add starting distance and starting wears. A wishlist can move into the collection
• Share a collection card or a month card made on your iPhone
• A calendar, and a nudge when a pair has been sitting
• Export a CSV. Optional reminders stay on the phone and are off until you turn them on

LEVEL UP, GENTLY
• Earn Sole Level XP for wears, distance, and badges, with named levels from First Steps to Sole Legend
• A three-step starter quest to get going, with its own badge
• A fresh weekly challenge, like wearing three different pairs or logging five wears

SOLE CHECK
• Take a photo of a pair's sole now and then and see a timeline with date and distance
• Compare the first and latest photo side by side to see how the tread is wearing
• A soft reminder every 100 km since the last sole photo

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking
• Everything stays on your iPhone, including Health numbers
• SoleCount is free. Every feature is included
• Not a medical app. Goals and tips are encouragement, not health advice
