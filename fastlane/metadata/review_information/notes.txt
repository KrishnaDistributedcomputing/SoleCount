Thank you for reviewing SoleCount.

SoleCount is not a medical app. Step goals, tips, badges, levels, and distance comparisons are general encouragement. They are not health advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment.

- No login or account is required. All data is stored only on the device (SwiftData). The app has no servers, no network requests of its own, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs.
- The app is free. There is no in-app purchase and no subscription.
- HealthKit is optional and read-only. The Info.plist includes NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription because Apple requires it with the HealthKit entitlement, but SoleCount never writes to Apple Health. It reads step count and walking/running distance for the days a pair was marked as worn. If several pairs were worn the same day, that day's steps and distance are split evenly. The app works fully if Health is denied or unavailable.
- Health data is not used for advertising, is not stored in iCloud, and never leaves the device. App Privacy is "Data Not Collected" because nothing is transmitted.
- Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): used only when the person taps "Take Photo" when adding a pair, or "Take a sole photo" in the Sole check section of a pair's screen (a photo of the shoe's underside to compare tread wear over time). If no camera is available, the system photo picker is used. All photos are stored on the device only.
- Settings > "Help make SoleCount better": "Send feedback" and "Suggest a feature" open the Mail composer (or a mailto: link, or show the address to copy) addressed to the developer. Nothing is sent automatically; the person must tap Send. The draft contains only the app version, iOS version, and device model. "Rate SoleCount" uses Apple's requestReview.
- To try HealthKit: allow the read prompt, mark a pair as worn today, then tap Refresh. On the simulator, add sample steps in the Health app first (Browse > Activity > Steps > Add Data).
- To test the core feature: on Today tap "Add my first pair" (or Collection > +), enter a name and price, open the pair, and tap "Wore today". Cost per wear, the Sole Level card, the starter quest, and the weekly challenge update. The Stats and Calendar tabs summarize the collection.
- Optional local notifications are off until the person turns them on in Settings. They are scheduled on the device only.
- Export: Settings > Export CSV uses the system share sheet.
- A Home Screen widget shows today's step ring. It reads a snapshot the app saved on the device. It does not read HealthKit itself.
- Export compliance: the app uses no encryption beyond what Apple's OS provides (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO).
