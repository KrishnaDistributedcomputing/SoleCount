Thank you for reviewing SoleCount.

SoleCount is not a medical app. Step goals, tips, badges, and distance comparisons are general encouragement. They are not health advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment.

- No login or account is required. All data is stored only on the device (SwiftData). The app has no servers, analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs.
- The app is free. There is no in-app purchase and no subscription.
- HealthKit is used to read steps and walking/running distance so the app can show kilometres (or miles) for the days a pair was marked as worn. If several pairs were worn the same day, that day's steps and distance are split evenly. SoleCount never writes to Apple Health.
- Health data is not used for advertising, is not stored in iCloud, and never leaves the device. App Privacy stays "Data Not Collected" because nothing is transmitted.
- To try HealthKit: allow the read prompt, mark a pair as worn today, then tap Refresh. On the simulator, add sample steps in the Health app first (Browse > Activity > Steps > Add Data).
- To test the core feature: open Collection, tap + to add a pair (name and price), open it, and tap "Wore today". Cost per wear updates. The Today, Stats, and Calendar tabs summarize the collection.
- Photos are chosen with the system photo picker and stay on the device.
- Optional local notifications are off until the person turns them on in Settings. They are scheduled on the device only.
- Export: Settings > Export CSV uses the system share sheet.
- A Home Screen widget shows today's step ring. It reads a snapshot the app saved on the device. It does not read HealthKit itself.
- Export compliance: the app uses no encryption beyond Apple's standard OS-provided HTTPS (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO).
