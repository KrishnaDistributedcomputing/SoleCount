# SoleCount Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

SoleCount does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- Everything you enter (sneakers, photos, prices, wear history, notes) is stored only on your iPhone.
- SoleCount has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- Photos you choose are copied into the app's own storage on your device and are never uploaded.
- If you connect Apple Health, SoleCount reads step count and walking-plus-running distance for the days you marked a pair as worn. That reading stays on this iPhone. It is not used for ads, it is not stored in iCloud, and it is not sent to anyone.
- SoleCount never writes to Apple Health.
- Optional reminders are scheduled on the iPhone. They are not sent through a server.
- A CSV export uses the system share sheet. The file leaves the app only if you choose to share it.
- The Home Screen widget shows a copy of today's steps that the app saved on this iPhone.
- Purchases are not part of this version. The app is free.
- App Privacy in App Store Connect stays "Data Not Collected" because nothing is transmitted off the device.
- If you delete the app, all of its data is deleted from your device.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/KrishnaDistributedcomputing/SoleCount/issues
